kavalo is a reader-supported editorial site. We research and test small-dog gear and link to retailers (such as Amazon and other stores) where you can buy it. We do **not** sell, stock, ship, or handle any products ourselves.

Because we never take possession of an order, **kavalo cannot issue refunds, process returns, or exchange items.** Those steps are handled entirely by the retailer you purchased from.

## How returns work

1. **The retailer is the seller.** When you click a link on kavalo and buy, your contract is with that retailer, not with us.
2. **Each retailer sets its own policy.** Return windows, eligibility, and refund timing vary. Many large retailers, including Amazon, offer return windows in the region of 30 days for eligible items, but the exact terms depend on the product category and the seller — always check the listing before buying.
3. **Start the return where you bought it.** Use your order confirmation email or your account on the retailer's site to request a return or refund.

## What we can and can't do

- **We can:** answer questions about our test methods, our recommendations, or a specific article; point you to the retailer's policy for a product we covered.
- **We can't:** access your order, issue a refund, start a return, or replace a damaged item.

## If something arrived damaged or defective

Contact the retailer's customer service directly. Most retailers offer a replacement or refund for items that arrive damaged or are defective on arrival.
