# Affiliate disclosure

<div class="lede">We believe you should know exactly how a review site makes money before you read a single recommendation. Here's the full picture — no fine print.</div>

<div class="rule"></div>

## The short version

Some links on kavalo.top are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to products we have actually tested on our own dog.

<div class="callout"> <strong>As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.</strong> This is the line Amazon requires us to show you. It means exactly what it says — nothing more, nothing less.
</div>

## What we do

- **We buy our own gear.** Every product reviewed on this site was purchased with our own money. We do not accept free products in exchange for reviews.
- **We use affiliate links where available.** If a product we've tested is sold on Amazon, Chewy, or another retailer with an affiliate program, we may link to it and earn a commission if you buy.
- **We link to the product we tested.** Not a "similar" one, not a "better" one — the actual model we put on Peanut and took outside.
- **You pay the same price either way.** Affiliate links do not change the price you pay. The commission comes from the retailer's margin, not from your wallet.

## What we don't do

- **No sponsored placements.** We have never accepted payment to rank a product higher, write a positive review, or include a product in a "best of" list. We never will.
- **No free products.** We have never accepted free products in exchange for coverage. Every item was bought retail.
- **No link manipulation.** We don't use cloaked links, redirect chains, or tracking pixels designed to hide where a link goes. Every affiliate link goes directly to the retailer's product page.
- **No fake urgency.** No "only 3 left in stock!" panic tactics. If a product is good, it's good tomorrow too.

## How to spot an affiliate link

Affiliate links on this site look like regular links — they go to Amazon, Chewy, or other retailer product pages. We don't mark them with special symbols because we believe every link should be equally trustworthy. If you want to check whether a link is an affiliate link, you can hover over it and look at the URL, or just assume that any link to a product on Amazon or Chewy is an affiliate link.

## Which affiliate programs we use

- **Amazon Associates** — 3% commission on pet products, 24-hour cookie
- **Chewy Affiliate Program** — commission on pet supplies (rate varies)
- Other programs may be added over time. We will update this page whenever we join a new program.

If we ever join a program that changes our editorial process in any way, we will say so here, in plain language, before we publish a single link.

## Our independence guarantee

Here is the most important thing on this page: **an affiliate link never changes what we write.** We test the product first, form an opinion, write the review, and then — if the product is worth recommending — we add the affiliate link. If a product is bad, we don't link to it, period. A bad product earns us zero commission because we don't link to it.

This means our income depends entirely on recommending good products. If we recommend garbage, you don't buy it, and we make nothing. That is the alignment we want.

## Questions?

If you have any questions about how we make money, what links are affiliate links, or why we recommend a product, email us. We'll answer honestly — even if the answer is "we got this one wrong."
