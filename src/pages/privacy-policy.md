# Privacy policy

<div class="lede">We collect as little data as possible. Here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how to make us stop.</div>

<div class="rule"></div>

## The short version

- We use a privacy-friendly analytics tool (Umami) to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
- We do not run third-party advertising networks that track you across the web.
- If you email us, we keep your email address only as long as needed to reply.

## Analytics

We use **Umami** (hosted at stats.kavalo.top) to understand which pages are read and how visitors find the site. Umami is a privacy-focused analytics tool that:

- Does not use cookies
- Does not collect personally identifiable information
- Does not track you across other websites
- Does not share data with advertising networks

The data collected includes: anonymous page views, rough geographic location (country level), referral source (which website sent you here), and device type (mobile or desktop). None of this can be used to identify you personally.

If you want to opt out of analytics entirely, you can use a content blocker or browser extension like uBlock Origin — it will block the stats.kavalo.top script without breaking anything on the site.

## Cookies

kavalo.top does not set any cookies itself. Our analytics tool (Umami) does not use cookies. If we add a newsletter signup or comments in the future, those features may set cookies, and we will update this page to explain them.

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links to Amazon, Chewy, or other retailers. When you click an affiliate link, the retailer's website may set cookies on your browser to track whether you make a purchase. These cookies are controlled by the retailer, not by us. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for details.

## Email

If you contact us by email, we keep your email address and message only as long as needed to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list unless you explicitly subscribe.

If we add a newsletter in the future, we will use a double opt-in process (you have to confirm your subscription by clicking a link in an email). You will be able to unsubscribe at any time, and we will not email you for any other reason without your permission.

## Third-party services

This website is hosted on a server we control. We do not use third-party CDN providers, tracking pixels, or advertising networks that collect data about you.

If you click a link to Amazon, Chewy, or another retailer, you are subject to that retailer's privacy policy — not ours.

## Children's privacy

This website is about small dog gear. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Your rights

If you are an EU or California resident, you have additional privacy rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively. Since we don't collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. But if you believe we have data about you and want it deleted, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will update the date below and explain what changed. We will never quietly reduce your privacy protections.

## Contact

If you have questions about privacy, email us. We'll answer honestly and promptly.
